What it does

Everything from inventory to install

One planner for the whole run-up to a show — connected directly to the Airtable base your inventory already lives in.

Airtable-native inventory

Titles, dimensions, pricing, condition, and consignment details stay exactly where you already keep them — Tradeshow just gives them a home on the wall.

True-to-scale wall planning

Drag, drop, and rotate pieces on a wall sized to your real dimensions — down to the inch — so nothing surprises you on install day.

Full 3D booth walkthroughs

Build your booth in 3D, assign walls to each surface, and place freestanding pieces on the floor — then walk through it from any angle before it exists.

Print-ready labels

Generate on-brand labels for every piece — your logo, your fields, auto-sized to fit — ready to print the moment you arrive.

Sales & consignment tracking

Mark pieces sold, calculate consignment splits automatically, and export a full report the moment the show closes.

Every show, kept organized

Booths, walls, and layouts stay tied to the show they belong to — reuse a layout for a recurring fair, or start fresh for a new venue in minutes.

How it works

From base to booth in three steps

Connect your base

Point Tradeshow at your existing Airtable inventory — nothing to re-enter.

Plan the booth

Lay out walls and floor pieces in 2D or 3D, to the exact dimensions of your space.

Print & track

Print labels for every piece, then track sales and consignments as the show runs.
